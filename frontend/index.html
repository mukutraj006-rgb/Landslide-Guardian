A software-only, explainable AI early-warning system designed specifically for the rugged terrain and high-rainfall regions of North-Eastern India.

System Pipeline & Methodology

1. Data Collection

Fetches live rainfall, precipitation intensity, elevation, and terrain slope from environmental APIs & datasets.

2. Normalization

Aggregates 1h, 3h, 24h accumulated rainfall, soil moisture indicators, and slope gradients.

3. Explainable AI

Scikit-learn Random Forest model computes probability and categorizes risk (Low, Moderate, High, Critical).

4. Citizen SOS Alerts

Dispatches emergency SMS alerts and dashboard warnings directly to registered citizens and response teams.
